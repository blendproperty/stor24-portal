## Customer notice

Blend Property 20 (Pty) Ltd, trading as STOR24, collects your ID or passport copy to check who is renting the unit and administer your storage agreement. Upload your own document. Authorised STOR24 staff check it against your booking; this is a manual review, not an automated authenticity check. Bring your original ID when collecting your keys.

Your uploaded copy is encrypted and can be viewed only by authorised reviewers for your store. ID documents in this workflow are not used for marketing or facial-access enrolment. Uploading an ID does not activate property access.

We keep the copy during your booking and throughout the resulting tenancy, including a notice period. The scheduled deletion process removes the application copy when the tenancy is recorded as closed or cancelled, or if the booking is cancelled, expires or is abandoned before signing. Replaced or withdrawn copies are removed from the application earlier. The document-review and security audit records are separate from the copy and remain retained. Backup expiry is separate; application deletion does not mean immediate erasure of every backup.

Uploading an ID is required before online agreement signing. If you cannot upload, need help, or want to request access, correction or deletion of your information, contact STOR24 at legal@blendproperty.co.za. Company representatives should contact the store about authority documents. Staff must still verify the original document and complete the handover checks before access can be activated.

## Configuration and boundaries

- `retentionMode: TENANCY`; no `retentionHours` or artificial expiry date. Each upload records its own mode and notice; prior fixed-period copies are never silently extended.
- Existing verified-email scope remains, across units and devices. The words test/sample are removed from customer collection copy. This is real ID collection for the authorised customer's bookings, not an organisation-wide activation.
- Staff can read retained accepted copies under existing identity-review permissions after handover, with every view audited. Onboarding replacement/acceptance actions are disabled after handover. Closed/cancelled tenancies cannot be previewed while waiting for scheduled deletion.
- Staff must record the actual tenancy closure; an intended end date alone does not terminate a tenancy. Open signed bookings awaiting handover remain retained until cancellation/conversion. No invented backup-expiry or legal-hold capability is claimed.
- This change does not enable live Netcash payments. The configured default connection remains test, and the public booking checkout is explicitly sandbox-only. A live booking payment path, live merchant setup and actual provider-return/receipt verification remain outstanding.
