# Posted recurring-charge history

14 October 2026. Reports > Posted recurring-charge history reads retained canonical monthly posting evidence, original invoice snapshots and original ledger lines. Use the effective-date range and permitted store filter. Online totals show verified original billed charges, included tax and the number of held invoices. CSV, JSON, XLSX and PDF are backup/report artifacts; historical billed lines do not select or submit collections.

The report never recalculates old amounts using current prices or plans. Original credits and tax retain their signs; invoice hashes, original account/document/period/fingerprint, SAST effective dates and posting totals must reconcile. Missing or changed evidence holds the invoice with no amount. Legacy components without a retained component key stay unclassified. Future monthly posts retain their actual component key, without changing financial calculations or assigning categories to old records.

Coverage is limited to retained canonical monthly postings, including closed tenancies. Billing effective dates and recorded dates are separate. Invalid-period evidence recorded in the selected range is shown for review; source limits require a narrower date/store scope. Absence of rows does not establish zero historical activity. Later corrections, receipt allocation, current debt, settlement and cash/accrual income recognition are outside these original billed amounts. Finance recognition, historical migration/completeness and provider reconciliation remain separate acceptance gates.

The existing current-plan recurring-charge report still supports its own reviewed direct Netcash batch workflow; this historical report cannot safely infer that original charges are still unpaid. Financial report permissions, store scoping, export permissions and existing data-protection controls remain authoritative. Automated fixtures use synthetic invoices and no provider transport. Production data, finance totals, staff UAT and protected release verification must be recorded separately in PROJECT_CONTEXT.md.

## Store identity in financial summaries

Current-plan and original-posted line records retain the permitted store id and business code. Summaries group by store identity rather than display name; identical names display their distinct store codes. Missing or conflicting attribution and unsafe monetary totals require source review. The preview shows a review message and clears old totals when source validation fails. No billing calculation, ledger balance, collection instruction, receipt or income-recognition rule is changed by this correction. Its protected promotion and database/runtime evidence remain separately recorded in PROJECT_CONTEXT.md.
