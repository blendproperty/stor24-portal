## Evidence needed for each confirmed recipient

Record service and legal entity, accountable internal owner, controller/processor roles and purpose; actual fields and systems; production status and transfer path; processing and backup locations; subprocessors and onward transfers; signed agreement and security, incident notification, retention, return/deletion and audit terms; applicable international-transfer grounds; and the owner/date for periodic review. Record evidence links and unknowns without copying secrets or personal records. Confirm privacy notices and consent settings match actual practice.

For privacy requests, document how each confirmed provider locates and handles an individual request, including backups and legally retained records; capture provider and completion evidence. For Clarity, the outstanding handling of data sent during consent withdrawal and the route for individual deletion remain unverified; no Microsoft contact is requested at this stage.

**Closure:** Information Officer/vendor management approves the completed register and matches agreements and transfer grounds to deployed integrations. Source review, a local test or a code change alone does not close PRIV-07. PRIV-06 rehearsals and provider follow-through also remain open.
