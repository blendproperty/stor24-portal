# STOR24 Six Month Marketing Plan

## Park tenants and nearby residential communities

Prepared for Brett Dovey and StoreSmart for review and execution planning

18 September 2026 | Proposed campaign period 21 September 2026 to 20 March 2027

Start building local awareness immediately. Make Midpoint park tenants the first business audience and nearby residents the first household audience. Convert that awareness through a staffed enquiry and viewing service, then increase advertising only as actual move-ins, available stock and acquisition economics justify it.

This plan turns the supplied brand strategy and presentation into a six-month operating programme. It covers audience selection, local outreach, paid and organic channels, content, partnerships, lead handling, retention, budgets, measurement, system requirements and a 26-week delivery schedule.

The recommended planning envelope is R240,000 over six months, including R115,000 in paid media. This is an allowance for discussion, not a supplier quotation or approved expenditure. The illustrative base case is 330 qualified enquiries and 66 new paying customers over six months, conditional on sufficient saleable inventory and operational readiness. These are testable planning assumptions, not forecasts or commitments.

The immediate priorities are a working branded website destination, a reliable enquiry-to-CRM process, verified opening information, an assigned responder and approved park outreach. Awareness preparation can start while payment, access-control and other launch gates are resolved.

### How to use this plan

Sections 1 to 5 explain the brief, source corrections, audience and launch requirements. Sections 6 to 11 provide each month's campaign. Sections 12 to 18 set channel, commercial and operational rules. Sections 19 to 22 contain the weekly schedule, responsibilities and evidence register.

All dates, budgets, output volumes, commercial offers and role assignments below are proposed unless specifically described as verified. This document does not authorise spend, publishing, customer contact, opening, payment collection or production changes. No external messages or campaigns were sent as part of preparing it.

### Six month objectives

- Establish recognisable STOR24 branding across approved park touchpoints and selected nearby residential communities.
- Reach every eligible business on the validated park tenant register through an appropriate approved channel, recording reach and response rather than assuming all tenants received a message.
- Build a repeatable flow of qualified enquiries from residents, local search and business referrals.
- Convert enquiries into viewings and verified paying move-ins while preserving approved prices, terms and access rules.
- Establish useful reviews, referrals and retention reporting from real customers.
- Produce an evidence-based decision on the next six months, including which audiences and channels deserve more money.

### Priorities and allocation of effort

For the first eight weeks, devote approximately half of local outreach effort to park businesses and their employees and half to nearby residents and referral partners. This is a staffing guide, not a mandatory media split. Separate these audiences in reporting so inexpensive park activity is not hidden inside residential advertising results.

Use Google Search to capture active demand, local Meta creative to introduce STOR24, and direct park and community relationships to establish familiarity. Keep students and wider Johannesburg audiences as small later tests; the supplied brief gives park tenants and nearby residents priority.

### Two operating modes

Before opening is confirmed, use factual introduction, request-information and register-interest messages. Do not publish an opening date, advertise immediate move-in or take customers through an unapproved payment journey. Maintain a dated interest list in the CRM with permission and a realistic next-contact date.

Once StoreSmart confirms readiness, switch to enquiries, viewings and available-unit campaigns. If the digital rental journey is still gated, use an approved staff-assisted process. A website booking or successful payment alone must not be described as permission to enter a unit.

## 4 Audiences and local coverage

### Park businesses and their employees

Use the park manager's current tenancy register to identify eligible companies, occupied buildings and the appropriate contact route. Validate it rather than copying logos from a website. Segment office users, stock-holding businesses, trades and project-based teams. Discuss archive boxes, excess stock, promotional material and equipment only within the approved permitted-goods policy.

The key message is “Make room for your business at Midpoint.” Offer a short space assessment and an arranged viewing. Keep business-account enquiries separate from employees who want household storage. An employer may circulate an approved notice through its own lawful channel; it should not hand over staff contact lists for marketing.

### Nearby residents

Begin with a proposed primary catchment around the actual entrance, approximately 0 to 5 km, and test a secondary 5 to 10 km catchment only after enquiry quality is visible. Distance bands are planning hypotheses, not measured travel times or guaranteed ad boundaries.

Halfway House and Halfway Gardens are initial areas to validate. Vorna Valley, Carlswald, Noordwyk and Waterfall are candidate neighbourhood tests, subject to actual drive routes, residential density, competition and affordable response. StoreSmart should mark exact complexes and routes on a map in week 1. Do not print “five minutes away” without testing the journey.

| Segment | Trigger and message | First channel and action |
|---|---|---|
| Movers and renters | A gap between homes; space while moving | Local Search, estate-agent handout, request a size recommendation |
| Renovating households | Furniture needs temporary space | Community notices, contractor referral, arrange a viewing |
| Decluttering families | Recover a spare room or garage | Local video and size-guide content, ask for advice |
| Park businesses | Stock or equipment crowds productive space | Tenant introductions and on-site appointments |
| Local small businesses | Seasonal stock and changing storage needs | Business Search and partner introductions |
| Students | Term transitions and household moves | Small test only after local demand is evidenced |

### Distribution controls

Secure permission for each complex newsletter, noticeboard, reception display or park sign. Record venue, placement date, quantity and QR/source code. Avoid unapproved gate distribution, driver distraction, unsolicited messaging and broad purchased databases. Community administrators may publish approved notices under their own rules; administrator permission is not consent to scrape members' details.

Partner and complex names are not invented in this plan. The week 1 output is a validated named list with owner, permission, opportunity and next action, held with appropriate access in CRM or the approved business contact register.

## 6 Month 1 Local introduction and reliable enquiries

Validate the full eligible tenant list and make a first approved introduction to each business by the end of the month. Prepare two audience landing experiences using existing business/personal pages where suitable. Evidence should show the branded destination working, a reconciled lead register, a named response owner, an approved fact sheet, the full eligible park list addressed and the first channel report. Planning case: 40 qualified enquiries and eight paying customers if trading-ready. If not open, report qualified interest and planned dates separately; do not count these as customers.

For households, use “Make space for the year ahead” with real decluttering and moving examples. For businesses, use a workspace reset message tied to excess stock, approved records storage or equipment. Ask customers about duration and access needs before recommending a unit.

Prepare 12 posts in advance, including four short videos, four profile updates and two articles: a household space reset and planning business storage for the year. Schedule around real operating hours. A content calendar can continue during leave, but replies still need human ownership.

## 12 Channel operating instructions

### Google Search

Separate branded demand from non-brand local storage demand. Begin with tightly grouped phrase and exact-intent terms around self storage Midrand, storage Halfway House, moving storage and business storage. Check actual query results before excluding ambiguous terms. Route each advert to the relevant page, with one clear next step and current factual information.

Start with people present in or regularly in the approved catchment, subject to the current account settings; Google also offers broader presence-or-interest targeting [S7]. Review the geographic report rather than assuming radius targeting is exact. Treat out-of-area relocation interest as a separate later test.

Show call actions during answered hours. Review search terms twice weekly, landing performance weekly and costs against qualified leads and paid customers monthly. Use conservative initial bidding with a documented spend ceiling until genuine conversion data is reliable. Do not optimise on page views or calculator clicks as if they were rentals.

### Meta and community content

Use Facebook and Instagram local creative with household situations and real facility proof. Test two concepts at a time. Click-to-message campaigns require a staffed, verified destination; native lead forms require a tested import into CRM, permission wording and response ownership. Keep the first test simple by sending users to the verified website or staffed enquiry channel.

Review audience quality, frequency and comments weekly. Refresh creative when repeated exposure coincides with declining qualified response. Avoid broad national reach or elaborate interest stacks before local evidence exists.

### Local search and useful content

Verify the correct Business Profile, category, name, address, map pin, telephone, hours and website. Use real photography and accurate service descriptions. Google's local guidance centres on relevance, distance and prominence [S6]. Record profile interactions and actual enquiries; profile views are not customers.

Build strong Midpoint, personal, business and moving pages. Add unique neighbourhood guidance only where it helps customers reach this real facility; do not fabricate branches or publish duplicated suburb pages. Maintain crawlable pages, clear titles, internal links and a sitemap. Check indexing through Search Console.

Google says normal SEO practices apply to its AI search features and no special optimisation is required [S8]. Budget for useful answers and reliable local information, not a separate guaranteed “AI ranking” product.

### Retargeting and deferred channels

After permissions and platform eligibility are confirmed, test recent engaged visitors and exclude converted customers where lawful and technically reliable. Suppress unsubscribed audiences. Do not upload tenant or residential contact lists merely because they exist.

YouTube paid media, broad display, paid influencers, radio and large outdoor buys are deferred pending measured value. A park screen can carry approved creative if available and permitted; screen existence, technical specification and a media booking are not assumed.

| Stage | Staff action | Evidence to record |
|---|---|---|
| New enquiry | Confirm receipt and preferred service contact method | Time, source, audience and assigned owner |

Use a qualified enquiry definition consistently: a real, contactable prospect with a stated storage need, plausible timing and a requirement the facility can serve. Deduplicate repeated calls, forms and messages from the same prospect about the same need. Keep raw enquiry counts separately.

Suggested enquiry follow-up: same-day advice, a next-working-day check on unanswered questions, then one further agreed check within three working days. Respect objections, opt-outs and the prospect's preferred timing. Transactional enquiry follow-up is not a standing subscription to advertising.

### Retention and reputation

Provide clear move-in and contact information. Conduct a day-3 service check and, where useful, a day-14 suitability check. Record actual review invitations consistently across genuine customers, including those who had complaints. Do not buy reviews, offer rewards for reviews or condition incentives on ratings [S9].

A proposed referral reward may be a modest fixed account credit or packing benefit, but its value, eligibility, payment trigger, expiry, tax treatment and abuse controls need approval. Pay only for a verified eligible referral, avoid self-referrals and record reward cost in acquisition economics. No reward is included as a promised customer entitlement in this plan.

For a complaint, acknowledge promptly, assign an owner and offer a factual resolution path. Escalate safety, access and money issues to operations. Do not expose a customer's tenancy or payment details in a public review reply.

### Commercial test

Finance should calculate contribution after concessions and incremental costs. For illustration only, R1,000 net monthly rental revenue at 65% contribution gives R650 per customer-month. R3,636 CAC would require about 5.6 occupied months to recover. At a three-month stay, contribution would be R1,950 and would not recover that acquisition cost. This example is not a claim about STOR24 rates or margins.

Use expected contribution over a conservative customer lifetime to approve CAC. A four-month payback rule in the illustrative example would permit at most R2,600 CAC. Until actual economics justify more, reduce acquisition cost or spend rather than celebrating a high lead count.

### Occupancy and business measurement

Closing occupied units = opening occupied units + actual unit move-ins − move-outs, with transfers and inventory releases reconciled. Paying customers and unit move-ins are different if a customer rents multiple units. Report unit occupancy and occupied square metres separately. Do not count holds, test accounts, unavailable units or future phases as occupied stock.

Set an occupancy target only after StoreSmart supplies the denominator, starting occupancy, expected move-outs and rentable-size mix. Treat the presentation's 85% day-90 figure as superseded for planning until that model exists.
