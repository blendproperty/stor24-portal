# Unidentified payment investigation and imported receipt posting

## Current source checkpoint — 15 October 2026

The existing intake and matching workflow remains separate from financial execution. Matching review alone posts no money. This branch adds incoming bank-source approval and an explicit imported-receipt posting workflow; it is still undergoing validation and has not been merged, deployed or used to post real customer money.

## Staff process

1. Retain the original evidence in Unidentified payment review and choose the correct store. Manually reported evidence remains unverified and cannot be posted through this workflow. Supported imported rows must match the retained encrypted original file, SHA-256 checksum, exact row fields and original amount/date. A complete Netcash statement must balance. Authorisation rows are not received cash.
2. For an incoming bank receipt, first request an Incoming bank sources review. Enter the original institution BIC and incoming account number privately; keep bank numbers out of labels and references. A different authorised colleague checks the original evidence and account identity before approval. This is manual finance approval, not provider account verification or payment authority. Stable physical identity prevents another alias or zero padding from creating another bank cash namespace. No bank identity is prefilled.
3. Import the normalised extract in Settlement reconciliation, select that approved source and retain the original source-declared ZAR currency column. Original transaction IDs, signs and dates remain unchanged. Old four-column and unmapped imports are retained for investigation or payout matching, but cannot fund a customer receipt. Foreign or guessed currency is refused. No automatic conversion or rounding is performed.
4. Suggest the exact same-store ZAR tenancy account and obtain independent matching review. Source/account changes require a fresh review. Then a person independent of the capturer and proposer, with current posting/review/payment/ledger/billing access in that store, performs the separate receipt posting review.
5. Record the finance approval reference and explicitly confirm the complete original source/currency/date/amount, that it is this customer's received payment, and that no receipt already records it. This last check includes other imports and Netcash: differing bank/provider references do not automatically prove that two rows represent different cash. Root deduplication prevents repeated posting of the same retained stable source; it cannot automatically correlate unrelated bank and merchant transaction namespaces.
6. Prepare and inspect the ten-minute signed preview: exact account number, amount, method and original receipt date. Post once only after explicit confirmation. The payment, ledger credit, private receipt document, source link and audit commit together or all roll back. The original cash date has DATE_ONLY precision; recording time is separate and no exact provider timestamp is invented. This endpoint calls no provider and sends no customer message.
7. After an uncertain response, reload saved receipt history and keep the original request reference. A read of the exact actor-bound saved request can confirm a committed outcome; the financial callback is never automatically replayed. A missing or inconsistent outcome remains a hold. Posted investigations and used bank/merchant sources cannot be withdrawn, voided or redirected to another payment. The receipt document is privately available from saved history. Later approved full reversal and same-store MOVE/partial UNDO preserve the original payment and cash date.

## Supported methods and controls

Netcash successful settled statement codes preserve card (including PNC/PVC/TDC/SDC), EFT, debit order/DebiCheck, retail, QR and supported Payflex/PayMyWay/voucher tenders. Authentication, mandate, return and business-funding codes have no customer-receipt fallback. Mapping follows [Netcash statement documentation](https://api.netcash.co.za/standard-integration/netcash-statement/); an imported statement does not initiate or authorise any provider operation.

Incoming account details use the existing encrypted vault, with masked workspace projection, protected private-detail read and audit. Immutable signed physical identity, retained independent approval evidence, current database grants, organisation/store scope, account/source locks, revision/source/account snapshots, stable source roots and unique request/payment/ledger/document constraints protect execution. New permission definitions do not assign grants to any employee. Native guides are read-only and match their permission intersections. A private receipt document does not bypass bulk personal-data export rules or send a message.
