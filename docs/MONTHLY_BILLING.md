# Monthly billing

Staff open Billing & payments → Monthly billing. `billing.view` permits scoped reads and previews; `configuration.manage` approves account terms; `billing.manage` posts reviewed bills. The owner can assign these permissions. A store-scoped role cannot access another store's account, policy or invoice.

Each account requires an explicit approved plan, including its first unbilled month and approval reference. Existing catalogue templates and Program defaults are not automatically applied to all customers. Account plans snapshot the actual agreed recurring charges and discount. Saving a template later does not alter a customer's contract. Insurance uses the enrolment's captured premium, not the current product price.

Amounts entered are gross/inclusive; tax treatment and rate must be approved by finance. Rent and insurance can use actual calendar days or full months. Occupancy end dates are exclusive (the transfer/move-out date belongs to the next occupancy, if any). Explicit recurring fees remain full monthly amounts. A full-month plan with multiple occupancies in the period requires review instead of charging multiple full rents. Discounts apply only to rent, within the approved month range, and cannot exceed rent. Overlapping occupancies block billing.

Previewing makes no financial writes. Posting rechecks the complete account, occupancy, insurance, plan, ledger and payment snapshot. Any intervening change requires a fresh preview. Account locking and a serializable transaction protect charges, balance, saved invoice and audit together. `RENT-YYYY-MM` remains the unique account/period marker, including compatibility with earlier rent-only runs. A second submission cannot bill the month twice.

Existing unclassified charges, credits or reversals in the requested period require review, including initial move-in charges. Historical sandbox contamination, missing insurance data, pending/closed tenancies, future periods and unsupported currency also block posting. No historical charge is deleted or silently replaced. Historical rate changes not represented by occupancy records require manual finance review; this release does not infer past contract rates.

Monthly invoices have a deterministic `INV-YYYYMM-{accountNumber}` identity and are generated in the posting transaction. They are saved HTML suitable for viewing/printing. A saved invoice is not proof of email delivery, collection or settlement. Posting sends no email, provider request or MRI journal.

The existing live cron retains its unchanged base-rent implementation by default. `MONTHLY_BILLING_AUTOMATION_ENABLED=true` switches it to the new approved-plan engine; `MONTHLY_BILLING_PAUSED=true` explicitly suspends all scheduled billing. Both switches default false. Do not switch engines until approved plans for all accounts, finance UAT, invoice acceptance and monitoring ownership are recorded. Both engines use the same account/month marker, so a manually posted new invoice cannot be rebilled by the legacy run. The enhanced engine checks every active/notice account, reports missing plans as exceptions and returns HTTP 409 if any account requires review; it does not silently omit unconfigured accounts. The current rollout preserves the live schedule without changing environment values.

Remaining financial approvals: opening balances/first unbilled periods, contract proration, charge and discount entitlement, tax treatment, insurer acceptance, historical corrections and document acceptance. Debit orders, settlement reconciliation, MRI journals and refunds are separate subsequent items.
