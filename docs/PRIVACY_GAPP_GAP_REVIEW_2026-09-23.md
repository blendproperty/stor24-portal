# STOR24 privacy and GAPP gap review

Review date: 23 September 2026. Status: review completed; remediation and legal acceptance OPEN. This is a scoped technical and operational gap assessment, not a legal opinion, certification, penetration test or approval to collect biometrics.

## Conclusion and scope

STOR24 has useful privacy safeguards, but does not yet have evidence sufficient to claim adherence across all ten Generally Accepted Privacy Principles (GAPP). The most urgent gaps are accessible collection notices, the lawful basis for compulsory facial access, and documented retention/deletion across all copies and providers.

Reviewed the deployed public site at https://stor4.srv938083.hstgr.cloud/ and the portal at https://stor24-site.srv938083.hstgr.cloud/, their booking/privacy-related source paths and release records. Public source baseline: `blendproperty/stor24` master `c3f9d1660a79755746153b0f7195f8d3b89774d8`. CRM source baseline: `blendproperty/stor24-portal` main `30cedda48ac315dc497d2f6ec1cca5cbf57d0f45`. These are source baselines, not claims that every source path is active in production. The CRM release record identifies deployed application `eec3417b679ce52a7136cb243ec04c5efed38d54`.

No forms submitted, customer photographs/IDs read, accounts changed, email sent, collection toggle changed, payment recorded or gate operation performed. Custom-domain equivalence, all cookies/network requests, contracts, officer registration and company-wide procedures were not independently verified. Missing evidence is distinguished below from observed implementation gaps.

A heartbeat proves execution, not that all applicable data and provider copies were deleted. ## Ten-principle assessment

| GAPP principle | Assessment | Evidence or remaining gap |
|---|---|---|
| Management | Operational evidence needed | Named accountability, registered Information Officer evidence, processing inventory, privacy risk assessments and staff training not supplied. |
| Notice | Confirmed gap | Public privacy/PAIA paths unavailable; collection notice absent from inspected booking form. Generic terms clause exists. |
| Choice and consent | Confirmed design gap | Mandatory facial enrolment uses consent with no configured alternative; four service channels preselected. |
| Collection | Partial | Basic booking data has an apparent purpose; necessity of full ID copies and raw facial photographs throughout tenancy needs documented justification. |
| Use, retention and disposal | Partial | Portal image erasure exists; provider/device, backup, metadata and stale-booking retention remain unresolved. |
| Access | Partial / evidence needed | Tenant documents and photo withdrawal exist; complete privacy-request handling is not evidenced. |
| Disclosure to third parties | Evidence needed | Processor inventory, contracts, locations, transfers and deletion obligations need verification. |
| Security for privacy | Partial | Encryption and access controls exist; operational assurance, incident exercises and broader testing remain open. |
| Quality | Partial | Staff verification exists; preferences and payment-driven access decisions require complete accuracy/recovery checks. |
| Monitoring and enforcement | Evidence needed | Application audit records exist; privacy complaint ownership, periodic review and closure evidence are not established. |

## Remediation checklist

All items remain unchecked. Proposed accountable roles below need Brett's assignment. Close an item only with linked implementation, test and operational/approval evidence; a policy draft or passing build alone is not acceptance.

- [ ] **PRIV-01 — High: publish accessible, accurate privacy and PAIA information.** Owner: Brett / legal reviewer, then website implementation. Approve the responsible entity/contact details, collection purposes, lawful grounds, mandatory versus optional fields and consequences, recipient categories, rights, complaint route, retention and applicable transfers. Publish public links in the footer and concise, specific notices before booking, ID and photo collection. PAIA content/requirements need legal review. **Close with:** approved version, anonymous desktop/mobile route checks, form-level links and verified contact routing. Evidence: public `app/components/Footer.tsx`, `app/components/booking/UnitFinder.tsx`; live observations above. CRM `src/lib/storage-terms.ts` section 18 supplies a useful general clause but not a complete collection notice.

- [ ] **PRIV-02 — High: resolve compulsory biometric collection before wider live rollout.** Owner: Brett / Liezl or appointed privacy adviser. `src/lib/facial-photo-control.ts` labels the policy INTERIM_AWAITING_REVIEW, calls enrolment required, offers no alternative and asks for consent. Consent must be voluntary; operational necessity alone does not establish the lawful basis or satisfy biometric safeguards. Decide and document the applicable legal basis, necessity/proportionality, special-information conditions, actual alternative if relying on consent, withdrawal consequences, minors and additional authorised users. **Close with:** signed review, approved notice and tested enrolment/decline/withdrawal paths. Recommend keeping collection off until this decision is resolved; this review did not turn it off or on. The owner toggle is an operational permission, not privacy approval. The previous suggestion to enable it for acceptance must not be treated as legal readiness.

- [ ] **PRIV-03 — High: separate required service contact from optional preferences.** Owner: product / privacy reviewer. All four booking channels default on. Necessary service delivery should have a clearly stated purpose/basis; optional channels and any future marketing must use appropriate genuine choices. Record the notice version, purpose, timestamp and source and provide a tested way to change preferences. Do not describe current service options as proven marketing violations. **Close with:** clean-session tests, persisted-choice checks for new and existing customers, withdrawal/change tests and no inappropriate channel sending. Evidence: public UnitFinder defaultChecked controls; CRM `src/lib/public-booking-service.ts` stores booleans/source/timestamp without a general privacy-notice version. `src/lib/public-lead-service.ts` also defaults email consent on; review it before enabling that quote path.

- [ ] **PRIV-04 — High: approve a data-specific retention and deletion schedule.** Owner: privacy / operations / engineering. Justify full ID copies and raw photographs for the entire tenancy rather than less data or shorter retention. Cover unsuccessful leads, abandoned/signed-but-never-moved-in bookings, ended tenancies, documents, invoices, contact preferences, access logs, image hashes, consent evidence and audit records separately. Current lifecycle erasure depends on appropriate reservation/tenancy states; an indefinitely active abandoned booking lacks a bounded interim expiry. Include lawful preservation exceptions. **Close with:** approved schedule plus representative expired, cancelled, withdrawn, rejected, no-show and ended-tenancy deletion tests. Evidence: identity-document-security, facial-photo-control and facial-photo-service modules; release documents.

- [ ] **PRIV-05 — High: prove deletion beyond the portal.** Owner: infrastructure / Hikvision provider / privacy reviewer. Deleting encrypted portal images does not prove deletion from Hikvision people records, biometric templates, devices, exports, logs or backups. Revoking access is not biometric deletion. Define backup expiry, restore suppression/re-deletion, provider confirmation and exception handling. **Close with:** end-to-end deletion evidence and a restore exercise, including provider/device confirmation. Evidence: `docs/FACIAL_ACCESS_RELEASE.md`, `docs/IDENTITY_DOCUMENT_RELEASE.md`, `docs/MEL_INTEGRATION_OWNERSHIP_MATRIX.md` row 9. Do not claim these external controls exist from application tests alone.

- [ ] **PRIV-06 — Medium-high: implement a complete privacy-request process.** Owner: Information Officer / customer operations. Customer access to selected documents and a photo-withdrawal button are useful, but are not a complete access/correction/deletion/objection process. Establish a public contact, proportionate identity verification, case register, applicable response deadlines, third-party follow-through and reasoned handling of records that must be retained. A manual controlled process can satisfy operational needs; a new dashboard is not inherently required. **Close with:** a rehearsed request across portal, providers and retained records, with acknowledgement and closure evidence. No company-wide process was supplied for verification.

- [ ] **PRIV-07 — Medium-high: map recipients and processor obligations.** Owner: privacy / vendor management. Inventory hosting, communications, payments, Google reCAPTCHA, Hikvision and any automation intermediary; verify actual use, processing locations, subprocessors, contracts, security/deletion terms and transfer grounds. Public quote source posts to n8n and copies to CRM, but its live exposure was not established: treat that flow as conditional until verified. **Close with:** approved data-flow/processor register and agreements matched to deployed integrations. Evidence: public quote API, booking/reCAPTCHA code and integration records. Do not infer every provider receives ID or biometric data.

- [ ] **PRIV-08 — Medium-high: evidence privacy accountability and incident readiness.** Owner: Brett / Information Officer. Verify officer appointment/registration and published contact, processing inventory, privacy risk assessment, staff confidentiality/training, least-privilege reviews and security-compromise response/notification procedure. Registration or procedures may already exist outside the repository; absence here is not proof of noncompliance. **Close with:** named ownership, records and an exercised incident/access-review process. Cross-reference P09/P10/P11/P13.

- [ ] **PRIV-09 — Medium: inventory cookies, browser storage and third-party scripts.** Owner: website / privacy reviewer. Review reCAPTCHA, session storage and any future analytics by purpose, lifetime and recipient; disclose accurately and implement choice before optional tracking where applicable. Source inspection found no obvious global advertising analytics tags; the space-guide analytics module emits a local event. A missing banner alone is not proof of unlawful tracking. **Close with:** fresh-browser network/storage inventory plus demonstrated controls for any optional trackers. Comprehensive network analysis was not performed in this review.

- [ ] **PRIV-10 — High before enabling payment-driven access restrictions: prove data quality and review safeguards.** Owner: finance / access operations / legal reviewer. The proposed seven-day period starts at confirmed debit failure, but final failure/settlement ingestion and provider suspension/restoration remain unbuilt. Establish correct customer/debt matching, resolved-payment updates, notice, dispute/manual review, audit and restoration. Assess POPIA automated-decision applicability rather than assuming every access decision falls under it. **Close with:** failed, reversed, paid, disputed, duplicate and delayed-provider cases, plus on-site restoration evidence. Training simulations do not establish live readiness.

- [ ] **PRIV-11 — Medium: make privacy monitoring repeatable.** Owner: Information Officer / operations. Maintain complaints and request registers, review notices/providers/retention on changes, track deletion exceptions and test access boundaries and incident readiness periodically. **Close with:** assigned cadence, alerts that reach an owner, review evidence and signed closure of this checklist. Existing audit logs and the deletion heartbeat are useful inputs, not complete programme assurance.

Relevant modules include `identity-document-security.ts`, `facial-photo-security.ts`, `facial-photo-service.ts`, `tenant-portal-security.ts` and `tenant-portal-response.ts` under `src/lib/`. ## Reference framework and legal context

- [AICPA/CICA GAPP, 2009](https://www.dataguidance.com/sites/default/files/aicpa_and_cica_generally_accepted_privacy_principles_2009.pdf): ten-principle assessment framework. GAPP is not a legal certification; AICPA later published the Privacy Management Framework.
- [POPIA, official Justice-hosted text](https://www.justice.gov.za/legislation/acts/2013-004.pdf): relevant provisions include sections 10–14, 18–24, 26–33, 55, 69, 71 and 72. Legal conclusions and applicable exceptions require the appointed reviewer's assessment.
- [Information Regulator proposed gated-access code, Gazette 54594, 30 April 2026](https://inforegulator.org.za/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/GG-NOTICE-CODE-OF-CONDUCT-ON-GATED-ACCESSES-WITH-COMMENTS-20.3.2026.pdf): explicitly published as a proposed code for comment. It flags conditional access/biometric consent concerns. Used here as draft guidance, not represented as binding final law or proof of its subsequent adoption.
